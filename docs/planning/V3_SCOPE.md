# V3 Scope — the Flavour Lab

The build specification for Recipe PWA V3. Every requirement traces to an ADR in
[`docs/adr`](../adr/README.md); the ADRs carry the reasoning, this document carries the work.

V1 and V2 are complete and shipped (PRs #1–#25). Nothing in V3 rewrites either. V3 adds one
thing the product cannot currently do: find and build **flavour per calorie**, so that
seasoning a cucumber competes with opening a packet of crisps.

Companions: [DOMAIN_MODEL.md](./DOMAIN_MODEL.md), [NUTRITION_MODEL.md](./NUTRITION_MODEL.md),
[UNIT_MODEL.md](./UNIT_MODEL.md), [DESIGN.md](./DESIGN.md),
[ARCHITECTURE.md](./ARCHITECTURE.md), [UX_CRITIQUE_V2.md](./UX_CRITIQUE_V2.md).

## Why this is small

The headline finding of the architecture review: **almost all of this already exists.** A
seasoning mix is a `Recipe` with derived nutrition. A snack is a `MealTemplate` that expands
onto a day. A jar of sauce in the fridge is a `Batch` with portions remaining. Logging one from
the Today screen shipped in ticket 9.

What is genuinely missing is narrower than it looks, and it is mostly **data and a query**:

| Missing | Fix | ADR |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The spices themselves — no paprika, cumin, turmeric, oregano, black pepper, MSG, miso, fish sauce, hot sauce in the shipped pack | A second, small, curated pack transcoded from USDA SR Legacy | [006](../adr/006-flavour-coverage-by-curated-second-dataset.md) |
| Every flavouring reads 0 kcal, so the product says salt is free | `sodiumMg` in the nutrition record, nullable | [007](../adr/007-sodium-as-a-nullable-nutrient.md) |
| Nobody weighs paprika in grams | Spoons as an entry-time conversion from a cited weight | [008](../adr/008-kitchen-spoons-as-an-entry-time-conversion.md) |
| No way to ask for "sour and spicy" | A closed sensory vocabulary on `Ingredient` | [009](../adr/009-sensory-tags-and-derived-constraints.md) |
| No surface that asks the question | One route, zero new entities | [010](../adr/010-flavour-lab-as-a-lens.md) |

No new Dexie table. No new entity. No change to any calculation already shipped.

## The headline acceptance scenario

Two budgets, because V2 measured that first-build and repeat-use behave completely differently
and a single number hid it ([UX_CRITIQUE_V2.md](./UX_CRITIQUE_V2.md) §Acceptance scenario
result).

> **First time.** From a fresh install, the user filters the Flavour Lab to *sour* + *spicy*
> under 10 kcal per teaspoon, builds **Chilli Garlic Vinegar** from five pack ingredients,
> and saves **cucumber + that vinegar** as a snack — in **under 2 minutes and fewer than 30
> interactions**, without typing a single nutrition figure.
>
> **Every time after.** From the Today screen, that snack is logged in **under 10 seconds and
> at most 4 interactions**, and the log shows calories **and** sodium.

The second budget is the one that decides whether the feature works. A snack that takes longer
than opening a packet does not replace opening a packet, and no amount of data quality
compensates.

**Known risk, carried from V2.** The composer needed ~18 interactions to build a four-item
meal, and the V2 critique attributed that to one-entry-at-a-time entry with a typed amount per
line. The Flavour Lab's "build a mix" surface must not reproduce that shape — see R5.4.

## Requirements

Each requirement has an ID for traceability. **MUST** is binding; **SHOULD** is expected
unless a specific obstacle is found and recorded.

### R1 — Flavour data coverage ([ADR-006](../adr/006-flavour-coverage-by-curated-second-dataset.md))

| ID | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| R1.1 | A second pack MUST be generated by a build-time transcode script from **USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy**, emitted to `public/starter-pack/flavour-pack.json` with `datasetId: "usda-sr-legacy"`. Nothing is transcribed by hand (R2.5, reused). |
| R1.2 | The pack contents MUST be driven by a **hand-maintained allow-list of FDC ids**, mirroring the `common-codes.json` pattern. Expect 60–120 entries. An id matching nothing in the source dataset MUST fail the build. |
| R1.3 | An allow-listed id that duplicates a food already seeded from CoFID MUST fail the build, checked against a hand-maintained equivalence map. **CoFID wins every collision.** |
| R1.4 | Every flavour-pack entry MUST be `common: true`. |
| R1.5 | Both packs MUST seed through the existing `seedStarterPack` path with the existing additive, `datasetId::entryCode`-matched, never-overwrite rules (R2.7). No second seeding mechanism. |
| R1.6 | `Settings` MUST gain `flavourPackVersion`, watermarked independently of `starterPackVersion`, and the existing *"Add missing starter ingredients"* action MUST top up both packs and report each separately. |
| R1.7 | Every entry MUST validate against `IngredientSchema` at build time, failing CI on any error (R2.9, reused). |
| R1.8 | The named gaps MUST resolve to exactly one ingredient each after seeding: paprika, smoked paprika, cumin, turmeric, cinnamon, oregano, black pepper, onion powder, MSG, miso, fish sauce, nutritional yeast, capers, balsamic vinegar, cider vinegar. This is the test that says the feature's premise holds. |
| R1.9 | USDA attribution MUST appear in Settings → About alongside the CoFID one. CC0 requests rather than requires attribution; we give it anyway. |

### R2 — Sodium ([ADR-007](../adr/007-sodium-as-a-nullable-nutrient.md))

| ID | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| R2.1 | `Nutrition` MUST gain `sodiumMg: number \| null`, on the same per-100 g / per-100 ml / per-item basis as every other figure. **`null` means unknown and MUST NOT be rendered, stored or summed as zero.** |
| R2.2 | `sum` MUST treat `null` as absorbing (any unknown contributor → unknown total) and `scale` MUST preserve it. `ZERO.sodiumMg` is `0`, so `sum([])` stays `ZERO`. |
| R2.3 | The existing monoid property tests MUST be extended over records containing nulls — identity, commutativity, associativity, and `scale` distributing over `sum` — not weakened or skipped for the new field. |
| R2.4 | The CoFID transcode MUST read the **`Inorganics` worksheet** alongside `Proximates`, taking sodium (mg/100 g) on the positional row alignment verified in [ADR-002](../adr/002-reference-ingredient-data-and-provenance.md) §2a. |
| R2.5 | Sentinels: `Tr` → `0`; **`N` → `null`, and the food is still seeded**; blank → `null`. This is the one place V3 diverges from R2.5d, and it diverges because `sodiumMg` is nullable where the macros are not. |
| R2.6 | The Dexie v3 migration MUST backfill `sodiumMg` on rows where `source.kind === "reference"` and `source.datasetId` matches a shipped pack, matched by `entryCode`. Every other row gets `null`. **No existing figure may be altered.** |
| R2.7 | Display MUST distinguish three states: known, partially known (*"at least 480 mg · 2 of 7 items unknown"*), and wholly unknown (*"Sodium not known"*). |
| R2.8 | Salt-equivalent MUST be derived for display, never stored (`salt g ≈ sodium mg × 2.5 ÷ 1000`). |
| R2.9 | Insights MUST show daily sodium against the ~2.4 g/day UK adult reference as **context, not a limit** — no enforcement, no warning, no red framing, in keeping with how the calorie target already behaves. |
| R2.10 | Batch snapshots written before this ships MUST keep `null` sodium forever. The history-immutability test MUST be extended to cover it. |

### R3 — Kitchen spoons ([ADR-008](../adr/008-kitchen-spoons-as-an-entry-time-conversion.md))

| ID | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| R3.1 | `Ingredient` MUST gain `gramsPerTsp: number \| null` and `gramsPerTbsp: number \| null`, populated by the flavour-pack transcode from the dataset's own portion rows. |
| R3.2 | Neither MUST be estimated, interpolated, or derived from the other. A source publishing only one leaves the other `null`. |
| R3.3 | `UNITS`, `Unit`, `MeasureKind` and `CanonicalQuantity` MUST be unchanged. **Spoons are not a unit.** No file in `domain/units` should need editing. |
| R3.4 | The quantity input MUST offer tsp/tbsp **only** when `measureKind` is `mass` and the corresponding field is non-null. No fallback default weight. |
| R3.5 | Entry MUST convert once at the boundary and store canonical grams, exactly as `kg → g` does. |
| R3.6 | `RecipeLine` MUST gain `entryHint: { spoons, spoon } \| null` for read-back. **No derivation may read it** — guarded by a byte-identical invariance property test, as `group` is by R4.3. |
| R3.7 | Per-spoon calories MUST be computed at display time. **No `kcalPerTsp` or `kcalPerTbsp` may be stored.** |
| R3.8 | Any nutrition derived from a spoon entry MUST be labelled approximate, as [UNIT_MODEL.md](./UNIT_MODEL.md) §Extension points requires. |
| R3.9 | The ingredient editor MUST accept user-supplied spoon weights, recorded with the user's own provenance. |

### R4 — Sensory tags and derived constraints ([ADR-009](../adr/009-sensory-tags-and-derived-constraints.md))

| ID | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| R4.1 | `Ingredient` MUST gain `flavourTags: FlavourTag[]` over the closed sixteen-value vocabulary. Empty is valid; null is not. A value outside the sixteen MUST fail validation. |
| R4.2 | Pack tags MUST come from a hand-maintained map keyed by `datasetId::entryCode`. A key matching no seeded ingredient MUST fail the build (the R2.5b rule, reused). |
| R4.3 | **No derivation may read `flavourTags`.** Guarded by a byte-identical invariance property test over a permuted corpus. |
| R4.4 | A mix's flavour profile MUST be derived as the union of its non-optional lines' tags. **It MUST NOT be stored.** |
| R4.5 | `calorieDense` (≥ 400 kcal/100 g), `highSodium` (≥ 600 mg/100 g) and `sodiumUnknown` MUST be pure predicates over existing figures, with thresholds defined once in `domain/`. No stored constraint field. |
| R4.6 | `highSodium` MUST be false when sodium is unknown; `sodiumUnknown` reports that case separately. |
| R4.7 | Tags MUST be editable in the ingredient editor. |
| R4.8 | Allergens, sweetener exposure, caffeine, iodine, acidity and GI tolerance MUST NOT be modelled — see ADR-009 §6 for why each was rejected. **No health score, no reward-per-calorie ranking.** |

### R5 — The Flavour Lab surface ([ADR-010](../adr/010-flavour-lab-as-a-lens.md))

| ID | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| R5.1 | `/flavour` MUST be one route in the **secondary** nav, page width role `content` (R1.2). It MUST create no Dexie table and own no entity. |
| R5.2 | It MUST filter ingredients by flavour tag (multi-select), kcal ceiling, sodium and `common`, showing kcal per 100 g, kcal per tsp where a cited weight exists, sodium or "not known", and the R4.5 markers. |
| R5.3 | Two tags MUST return the intersection. An ingredient with unknown sodium MUST be excluded from a sodium ceiling filter rather than treated as zero. |
| R5.4 | *Build a mix* MUST support **rapid multi-pick with a sensible default quantity per line**, editable but not required. This is the explicit correction of the V2 composer finding; a mix of five flavourings must not cost five mini-forms. |
| R5.5 | Saving a mix MUST create an ordinary `Recipe` with `kind: "mix"`, openable and editable in `/recipes`. Running nutrition while building MUST use the existing `recipeTotal`. |
| R5.6 | `Recipe` MUST gain `kind: "dish" \| "mix"`, defaulting to `"dish"` on migration, editable in the recipe form, and **read by no derivation** — same invariance test family. |
| R5.7 | *Save as a snack* MUST create an ordinary `MealTemplate` pairing a base `ingredient` component with a `recipeServings` component. **No recipe nesting** (decision 4). |
| R5.8 | Copy MUST state up front that a mix is a recipe and a snack is a saved meal, rather than hiding it behind a bespoke editor. |
| R5.9 | Filter controls MUST follow [ADR-005](../adr/005-choice-controls-by-cardinality.md)'s cardinality rule. Sixteen tags is the 7–15+ band: a button grid or command palette, **not** a segmented group, and verified at 320px (R5.9 of V2, still binding). |

### R6 — Example content

| ID | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| R6.1 | A small set of example mixes and snacks (roughly 6 mixes, 4 snacks) MUST be available via an **explicit Settings action**, not seeded automatically. Ingredients arrive unasked because nothing works without them; recipes are the user's own space. |
| R6.2 | Examples MUST be ordinary `Recipe` and `MealTemplate` rows — editable, archivable, deletable, with no special tier. |
| R6.3 | Every example MUST be composed **only** of seeded pack ingredients, so that no example carries a hand-typed nutrition figure. |
| R6.4 | Adding examples twice MUST NOT duplicate them, and MUST NOT overwrite a user's edits to one. |

## Data model changes

Full shapes and invariants live in the ADRs and in [DOMAIN_MODEL.md](./DOMAIN_MODEL.md).

| Entity | Change | Migration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Nutrition` | `+ sodiumMg: number \| null` | `null` everywhere, then reference rows backfilled from the packs (R2.6) |
| `Ingredient` | `+ gramsPerTsp`, `+ gramsPerTbsp`, `+ flavourTags` | `null`, `null`, `[]` |
| `RecipeLine` | `+ entryHint: SpoonEntryHint \| null` | `null` |
| `Recipe` | `+ kind: "dish" \| "mix"` | `"dish"` |
| `Settings` | `+ flavourPackVersion: string \| null` | `null`, via the existing `normalizeSettingsRow` pattern |

No new table. `MealTemplate`, `Batch`, `PantryStock`, `PlannedMeal`, `LoggedMeal`,
`MealSlot`, `ShoppingOverlay` and `IngredientCategory` are untouched.

### Dexie schema version 3

```
ingredients   id, name, categoryId, archivedAt, *flavourTags
recipes       id, name, archivedAt, kind
```

Every other store is unchanged from v2.

**Ticket 1 declares the whole of version 3, including the parts it does not use.** The
`*flavourTags` multi-entry index and the `kind` index are created by ticket 1 and sit unused
until tickets 4 and 5 give them behaviour.

This is the V2 lesson applied rather than relearned. `applySchemaVersions` runs a version once
per browser and a released version is never edited, so a partial v3 would record every user
who opened the app between ticket 1 and ticket 5 at v3 — and the later ticket extending v3
would then never run for them. Declaring an index costs nothing while it is empty; discovering
this after a deploy costs a v4 and a repair path.

Notes for the implementer:

- `*flavourTags` is Dexie multi-entry syntax and indexes each array element.
- `sodiumMg` lives inside the `nutrition` object and is **not** indexed. Filtering by sodium is
  an in-memory pass over a few thousand rows, which is cheaper than maintaining a nested index.
- `Backup.formatVersion` stays **1**; `Backup.schemaVersion` becomes **3**. Import must accept
  v1 and v2 payloads and run the normal migrations, and must still refuse a newer version.
- The append-only migration rule holds: v1 and v2 are never edited.
- `normalizeIngredientRow`, `normalizeSettingsRow` and friends gain the new fields, as they did
  in V2, so a backup import from an older schema is defensive as well as migrated.

## Testing

Additions to the tiers in [ARCHITECTURE.md](./ARCHITECTURE.md). The first two must not be
skipped.

1. **Null-sodium algebra (R2.2–R2.3)** — the governing test for ticket 1. Identity,
   commutativity, associativity and distribution all hold over records containing nulls; one
   unknown contributor makes the total unknown; `sum([])` is `ZERO` with `sodiumMg: 0`.
2. **Backfill safety (R2.6)** — a `reference` row gains its pack sodium; a `userEntered` row, a
   `packaging` row and a diverged reference row keep `null` and are otherwise byte-identical.
   No macro figure changes anywhere.
3. **Metadata invariance** — extend the existing permutation tests so that `flavourTags`,
   `entryHint` and `Recipe.kind` each leave requirements, shopping, availability, nutrition,
   `expand()` and every insight byte-identical. This is the same property that already guards
   `source`, `common` and `group`; it is now a family of six.
4. **History immutability, extended (R2.10)** — a batch snapshot written before the migration
   is byte-identical after it, including its null sodium. The V1 canary test, unchanged in
   spirit.
5. **Flavour-pack integrity (R1.2–R1.3, R1.7)** — a listed id resolving to nothing fails the
   build; an id overlapping a CoFID food fails the build; every entry validates; **every**
   entry's kcal and `entryCode` spot-checked against the published figures (affordable here
   because the pack is small).
6. **Coverage assertion (R1.8)** — the fifteen named gaps each resolve to exactly one seeded
   ingredient.
7. **Sentinel handling (R2.5)** — against named real entries, a `Tr` sodium seeds as `0`, an
   `N` seeds as `null`, and the `N` food is still seeded.
8. **Spoon conversion (R3.5)** — 2 tsp at 2.3 g/tsp stores `{ amount: 4.6, kind: "mass" }`; the
   option is absent when the weight is null, when the ingredient is not `mass`, and for `tbsp`
   when only `gramsPerTsp` exists.
9. **Derived mix profile (R4.4)** — equals the union of non-optional line tags and changes when
   an ingredient is retagged; optional lines excluded.
10. **Predicates (R4.5–R4.6)** — `calorieDense` true for tahini (607 kcal) and false for
    vinegar (22 kcal) on the seeded figures; `highSodium` false when sodium is unknown.
11. **Worked example end to end (R5.5, R5.7)** — build Chilli Garlic Vinegar at 3 servings, save
    a snack of 200 g cucumber plus 1 serving, apply it to today's snack slot, log it, and assert
    the logged nutrition equals cucumber plus one third of the vinegar batch, sodium included,
    with no figure typed by hand. The V3 counterpart of `acceptance-scenario.test.ts`.
12. **Migration and backup at v3** — a V1 and a V2 database each open at v3 with correct
    defaults and no nutrition figure changed; export → wipe → import round-trips with `null`
    preserved as `null`; a v4 payload is refused.
13. **Idempotent example content (R6.4)** — adding examples twice adds nothing the second time
    and never modifies an edited row.
14. **Filter controls at 320px (R5.9)** — the tag control wraps without truncating and stays
    ≥ 44px, the V2 release gate applied to the new surface.

Every V1 and V2 test must continue to pass unchanged, including
`src/features/today/acceptance-scenario.test.ts`.

## Explicitly not in V3

Recorded so no ticket reopens them.

**Carried forward unchanged** from decision 24 and V2's exclusions: recipe-to-recipe nesting ·
`batchPortions` in a meal template · online food-database lookup and barcode scanning ·
automated brand onboarding · third-party photos of generic foods · any setting controlling
control style · expiry, lots and waste tracking · multi-user, sync, accounts · macro targets ·
planned-versus-actual comparison · custom meal slots.

**New to V3:**

- **A separate Flavour Lab entity graph** — no flavour-specific ingredient, mix, sauce or snack
  table. ADR-010's first sentence is the boundary.
- **Allergen data** — a partial allergen list is more dangerous than none. Needs its own ADR, a
  complete source and explicit UI framing (ADR-009 §6).
- **Sweetener exposure, caffeine, iodine, acidity and GI-tolerance fields** — health claims or
  nutrients. If a nutrient is genuinely wanted, it arrives the way sodium did: a nullable field
  with a cited source.
- **A health score, or any single "reward per calorie" ranking** — preserve the attributes and
  let the user trade them off.
- **Scoville or numeric heat ratings** — no dataset carries them, so they would be invented.
- **`Recipe.yield` as a quantity** — sauces express yield through `servings`, and the
  limitation is stated in ADR-010 §4 rather than worked around. A real model change, deferred.
- **Density and cross-family (ml ↔ g) conversion** — decision 2 stands. ADR-008 adds a
  *cited, mass-only, entry-time* spoon weight and is not a density model.
- **A separate flavour pantry or favourites system** — the pantry holds raw ingredients
  (decision 13); a saved mix is a recipe and recipes are already listable.
